# Business Rules

Version: 1.0.0

Status: APPROVED

---

# BR-001

Every activity belongs to exactly one organizer.

---

# BR-002

An archived activity cannot become active again.

A copy must be created instead.

---

# BR-003

Only participants may access activity chat.

---

# BR-004

Organizer may define moderators.

Moderators have configurable permissions.

---

# BR-005

GPS sharing begins only after explicit user confirmation.

---

# BR-006

Arrival notifications are generated automatically.

Users cannot manually trigger arrival events.

---

# BR-007

Deleting an activity never deletes another activity's archive.

---

# BR-008

Changing activity type does not affect archived data.

---

# BR-009

Users may leave an activity at any time.

---

# BR-010

Every significant action is recorded in the activity timeline.

Examples

Ride created

Poll added

Route changed

Participant joined

Stage changed
